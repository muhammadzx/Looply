GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Let's clear things up.

A few things to know before your business gets started.

Still have a question? Contact us →
What is Looply?

Looply is a B2B marketplace designed to connect businesses that have surplus products, materials, or equipment with businesses that need them.

Who can use Looply?

The platform is intended for businesses such as manufacturers, wholesalers, suppliers, construction companies, and other commercial buyers and sellers.

Can my business buy and sell?

Yes. A business can use the same account to discover resources and publish its own listings.

How do prices and negotiations work?

Buyers can request a quote. Sellers can respond with offers, and both parties can negotiate terms before agreeing on a deal.

Does Looply process payments or deliveries?

The initial version organizes listings, quote requests, offers, and deals. Integrated payments and automatic delivery tracking are outside its initial scope.

How does the AI Listing Assistant work?

It is designed to suggest titles, categories, descriptions, and keywords. Businesses review and edit these suggestions before publishing.
